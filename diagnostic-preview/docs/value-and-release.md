# Delivering useful value without overstating measurement

The product goal is that a learner leaves with a clearer understanding of their English and usable next steps. A proposed 5,000 KZT price is not evidence of a 50,000 KZT market value; perceived value should be learned from pilot users rather than presented as a made-up discount.

## Proposed 5,000 KZT complete service

1. Online profile of receptive skills and language use, with uncertainty stated separately for every domain.
2. A teacher-led speaking sample with additional probes around the uncertain boundary, scored using a descriptor-based rubric.
3. An independent writing task and teacher feedback on task completion, organization, range and control.
4. A consolidated report showing observed strengths, specific examples, priorities and areas where evidence is still missing.
5. A realistic starter practice plan, chosen around the learner's goals and actual responses.
6. A personal explanation of suitable learning options, including independent practice. Course enrolment is optional.

The current prototype delivers the draft online experience, answer explanations and starter plan. Teacher assessment, calibrated rubrics, bookings, delivery timing and payment fulfilment still need to be established. Confirm staffing capacity before advertising a turnaround time.

## Research before precision claims

Follow the blinded, matched-domain pilot in `validation-status.md`. Measure boundary errors, unresolved results, consistency across forms, completion, and the usefulness of the report. Check whether learners can explain their next steps after reading it. Measure voluntary course enquiries separately from assessment scoring; never lower someone's level to improve sales conversion.

Do not equate a large question count, a long test, adaptation, a polished report, or a passing software test suite with validity. More items help only if they add appropriate, independent evidence and do not create unnecessary fatigue.

Use the CEFR construct and descriptor alignment process for English. Gallup is an example of sustained assessment research, not a universal test certification that can be applied to this product. Do not copy proprietary personality items, claims, report formats, or reliability coefficients.

## Commercial release gates

- A reviewed content blueprint and sufficiently broad original item bank; all unresolved multiple-key items removed.
- Versioned natural audio and transcript checks; assessment conditions and accessibility arrangements defined.
- Independent standard setting and human data sufficient for the specific placement decisions being advertised.
- Held-out evaluation against matching receptive-skill comparators, plus independent productive-skill ratings; release criteria set before viewing evaluation outcomes.
- A clear uncertainty/teacher-review path for every unsupported boundary; no unsupported fifteen-sublevel promise.
- A completed teacher service with agreed scope, capacity, report delivery, rescheduling, and support.
- Confirmed Kaspi merchant workflow and server-verified payment handling, with no secret keys in the static site.
- Transparent price, what is included, applicable refund/support terms, and minimum necessary personal data handling.
- End-to-end checkout, cancellation, duplicate notification, interrupted attempt, cross-device access, report delivery and refund testing on the chosen production host.

If the evidence supports only a broad placement orientation, sell and describe that narrower service honestly together with teacher assessment. Raise the precision of claims only when the evidence improves.
